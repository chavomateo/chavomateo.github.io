The Madrid Summit boosts Ibero-American cooperation on artificial intelligence

5 November 2026

The 30th Ibero-American Summit, held in Madrid, has adopted an intergovernmental cooperation mechanism on artificial intelligence built on three pillars: data, models in the region's languages and computing capacity. The Ibero-American AI Alliance sees it as a solid basis for bringing AI to startups and SMEs.

The first Ibero-American Ministerial Meeting on Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence agreed on a regional AI cooperation mechanism in Madrid on 3 November. On 4 and 5 November, the 30th Ibero-American Summit of Heads of State and Government adopted it in its final declaration and entrusted its implementation to the Ibero-American General Secretariat (SEGIB). Each country's participation is voluntary.

The intergovernmental AI mechanism agreed at the Summit is organised around three pillars. The first is an Ibero-American Data Space with a federated approach, in which each country decides what information it shares and on what terms. The second is the collaborative development of language models and AI systems in Spanish, Portuguese, other official languages and indigenous languages, building on initiatives that already exist in the region. The third is cooperation on computing capacity: organising available resources and matching supply with project demand, without building new regional infrastructure.

The Ibero-American AI Alliance believes the agreement gives the region a solid basis. That framework creates value when it reaches businesses, and this is where the Alliance contributes as a public-private initiative that shares that goal: bringing AI to startups and SMEs through replicable use cases, training and support.

For the new framework to translate into adoption, the Alliance puts forward three priorities: that the federated data space be built on interoperability and portability; that access to computing capacity also reach startups and SMEs, under neutral criteria that allow public infrastructure and commercial clouds to be combined; and that models in the region's languages come with real use cases developed by the entrepreneurial ecosystem.

The Ibero-American AI Alliance shares the goal agreed at the Madrid Summit. Within our remit, we work to bring artificial intelligence to the region's startups and SMEs, and we welcome governments and institutions that wish to join the Alliance.

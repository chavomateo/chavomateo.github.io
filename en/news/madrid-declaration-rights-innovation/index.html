Rights and innovation go hand in hand: the Madrid Declaration

5 November 2026

The Madrid Declaration, adopted by the Ibero-American ministers for Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence, states that innovation and the effective protection of rights are not opposing goals, but complementary ones. The Ibero-American AI Alliance has shared this approach since it began.

The first Ibero-American Ministerial Meeting on Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence, held in Madrid on 3 November as part of the 30th Ibero-American Summit, adopted the Madrid Declaration. The text holds that innovation, freedom, security and the effective protection of rights are not opposing goals, but complementary conditions for building trust, encouraging the adoption of AI and extending its economic and social benefits.

The ministers also agreed to promote entrepreneurship, investment, competition and the responsible adoption of AI, with public policies and regulatory frameworks geared towards clarity, proportionality and technological neutrality, in line with each country's priorities.

Having governments on both sides of the Atlantic endorse this principle strengthens the approach the Ibero-American AI Alliance stands for: clear and proportionate rules that protect people without holding back innovation or the competitiveness of startups and SMEs.

To make this principle real in practice, the Alliance proposes interoperable frameworks between Ibero-America and Europe, testing environments that startups can access, technological neutrality and freedom to choose a provider. This is the path to open strategic autonomy, built on the region's own capabilities, competition and interoperability, without closing markets.

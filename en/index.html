The challenge

Ibero-America has the talent. Europe, the framework. Apart, neither one leads.

The future of AI is being decided among a handful of blocs. Ibero-America and Europe share a language, a set of values and a common urgency: to adopt Artificial Intelligence without surrendering sovereignty or competitiveness. Yet two gaps hold both shores back —and neither can be closed alone.

01

A maturity gap in AI ecosystems.

Talent, capital, real-world adoption, infrastructure and data advance unevenly and at different speeds on each shore. The bridge accelerates maturity: critical mass, shared scale and use cases that travel from one shore to the other.

02

A strategic-autonomy gap.

We depend on the major poles for compute, models, cloud and standards. The bridge builds sovereignty: our own capacity to decide over our data, our infrastructure and our rules, grounded in shared democratic values.

Digital divide between the shores → Regulatory fragmentation → Talent drain beyond the axis → Technological dependence → Public–private disconnect → Ethical risks without governance

No maturity without scale, no sovereignty in isolation. Neither shore solves this alone. That is why the Alliance exists: to build the bridge.

Who we are · Manifesto

We believe Artificial Intelligence must serve people and sustainable development.

We believe innovation knows no borders: it calls for transatlantic collaboration.

We believe Europe and Ibero-America share one idea of AI: centred on people, rights and the common good.

We believe in AI with sovereignty: our own infrastructure, our own data, our own rules.

We believe that, together, the two shores reach the scale and the sovereignty that neither holds apart.

We believe neither shore should be left behind in the AI revolution.

That is why the Alliance exists.
